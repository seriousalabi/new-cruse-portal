# Official class and subject setup

The primary curriculum below is supplied by New Cruse Academy. Subject offerings are class-specific. Nursery and Reception subject offerings remain configurable until the school supplies them.

| Class | Subjects |
|---|---|
| Primary 1 | Mathematics; English Studies; Basic Science; CRS; PHE; CCA; Social & Citizenship Studies; Nigerian History; Hausa; Right Speech |
| Primary 2 | Same as Primary 1 |
| Primary 3 | Same as Primary 1 |
| Primary 4 | Mathematics; English Studies; BST; CRS; PHE; CCA; Social & Citizenship Studies; Nigerian History; PVS; French; Hausa; Right Speech |
| Primary 5 | Same as Primary 4 |

The seed data uses the consistent display forms `CRS`, `CCA`, and `PVS`, and title case for subject names. `BST` and `PVS` remain abbreviations exactly as supplied; their expanded names are not assumed.

## Grading

- Four assignments, each scored from 0.00 to 10.00.
- One examination, scored from 0.00 to 60.00.
- Scores and totals support two decimal places. Total is out of 100.00.
- A total from 0.00 through 40.00 inclusive is failing.

The exact start/end dates for the three terms and the school's official year labels are still to be supplied. The application will let authorized school staff configure these dates.
